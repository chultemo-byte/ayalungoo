Ayalungoo is a swimming school that teaches the thing most apps skip: why the water holds you. Amita is the teacher on this page. Boundless — that is her name.

Lesson One

Water pushes up with the weight of the water your body moves aside. That is the only law. Not courage. Not muscle.

Fb = ρ × V × g

Your lungs are the swim bladder. A quiet full inhale adds four to six litres of almost-weightless air. Fear empties the chest. That is why fear sinks.

Head is the rudder. Chin up, hips drop. Ears in the water, eyes to the sky, hips rise.

Stroke is a song. Stillness is the first note. Talk to Amita.

Amita

Enter water as a guest. Children need a grown-up in the water. Amita is a teaching role, not a doctor. If there is pain or panic, get out and find a person.
